Security & privacy

Your PDFs are processed and deleted after analysis.

We don't keep the files. We store an encrypted case index (key facts, short quotes and page references, and text excerpts used to compare documents) so your firm can find contradictions across filings. AI analysis is performed by Google Gemini under paid API terms (no training on your data; limited-period abuse logging). Large files are sent to Google’s Gemini file service for analysis and deleted immediately after; at most 48 hours if deletion fails. Short quotes from proposed findings are also sent to Anthropic’s Claude under its commercial API terms (no training on your data) to double-check findings before you see them. Only signed-in members of your firm can access your index.

Where files live

Lasting PDF copies stay with your firm. We do not run a central document library — analysis jobs hold file bytes only for the scan, then delete them.

What we store (and what we delete)

PDFs are deleted after analysis. Encrypted facts, findings, short quotes, and reports persist per matter until you delete them. Per-firm keys wrap sensitive columns (operator-held master key).

What we never do

We don't build a searchable archive of client documents, sell or share file content, or auto-file, auto-notify, or auto-escalate any finding to a third party.

Analysis tiers and what they send

Not every check needs the full exhibit. Deeper contextual analysis is opt-in, per file.

TierWhat runsWhat is sent for analysis
Tier 1 — Core forensics Metadata, PDF integrity, structural, image forensics, OCR/text, and entity/timeline checks. The file bytes for that scan job only, then deleted with the job.
Tier 2 — Entity verification Generated yes/no claim questions checked against public record (e.g. "did this entity operate in that market on that date?"). A short generated question — never the document itself beyond the scan job.
Tier 3 — Contextual document analysis Comprehensive cross-document reasoning over the full exhibit text and layout. The exhibit file itself (inline for small files; Google’s Gemini file service for large PDFs), then deleted immediately after; at most 48 hours if deletion fails.
Finding verification A second AI (Anthropic Claude) rereads each proposed finding and its evidence and drops findings that don't hold up, then rates how much each one matters to the case. The short claim and evidence quotes for each proposed finding — never the whole file.

The software default leaves Tier 3 off. The hosted CaseSifter service turns it on: non-court-filing exhibits that need a full visual review are sent to Gemini, then the source file is deleted. Court filings are not sent as native files. Do not upload sealed, privileged, or otherwise restricted evidence unless your court's rules allow that transfer.

Transport security

Public traffic uses modern TLS only — TLS 1.2 minimum, TLS 1.3 preferred (there is no “TLS 2.0”). Browsers get HSTS so cleartext is refused after the first HTTPS visit.

HTTPS everywhere

App, API, and marketing sites terminate TLS at the edge with current cipher suites. Cookies that carry a session are marked Secure and HttpOnly.

Hardened responses

Strict framing, content-type sniffing blocks, CSP, and cross-origin isolation headers reduce clickjacking and script injection surface.

Encrypted at rest (facts)

Matter facts and excerpts are wrapped with per-firm keys. Source PDFs are deleted after analysis — lasting copies stay with your firm.

How findings are kept honest

The same controls that protect accuracy also limit what a model-sourced finding can do to a report.

Confidence discounting

AI-sourced findings are capped at medium severity, have confidence discounted roughly 30%, and are hard-capped at 60% confidence — never enough, alone, to turn a report red.

Labeled, not hidden

Every AI-generated finding is explicitly marked as such in the report. You always know whether a flag came from deterministic forensics or a contextual model call.
