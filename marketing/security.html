Security & privacy

No document vault. That's the whole pitch.

CaseSifter does not keep a copy of your case files. The lasting record stays on your firm's machine; anything sent for analysis is processed and then deleted. Here's exactly how data moves, tier by tier.

Where files live

Case PDFs, the case index, and generated reports are stored in a CaseSifter folder on your desktop. There is no central document library on our servers.

What "ephemeral" means

A scan uploads the file bytes to an analysis job, processes them through the pipeline, and deletes the job directory on completion or after a time-to-live sweep — whichever comes first.

What we never do

We don't build a searchable archive of client documents, sell or share file content, or auto-file, auto-notify, or auto-escalate any finding to a third party.

Analysis tiers and what they send

Not every check needs your document to leave the building. Deeper contextual analysis is opt-in, per file.

TierWhat runsWhat leaves your machine
Tier 1 — Local forensics Metadata, PDF integrity, structural, image forensics, OCR/text, and local entity/timeline checks. Nothing. Runs against the file directly.
Tier 2 — Entity verification Generated yes/no claim questions checked against public record (e.g. "did this entity operate in that market on that date?"). A short generated question — never the document itself.
Tier 3 — Contextual document analysis Comprehensive cross-document reasoning over the full exhibit text and layout. The exhibit file itself, for that one analysis call, then deleted.

Tier 3 is opt-in and off by default. For sealed, privileged, or otherwise restricted evidence, confirm your court's rules before enabling it on that file.

How findings are kept honest

The same controls that protect accuracy also limit what a model-sourced finding can do to a report.

Confidence discounting

AI-sourced findings are capped at medium severity, have confidence discounted roughly 30%, and are hard-capped at 60% confidence — never enough, alone, to turn a report red.

Labeled, not hidden

Every AI-generated finding is explicitly marked as such in the report. You always know whether a flag came from deterministic forensics or a contextual model call.

Have a specific compliance question?

Court rules on evidence handling vary. Tell us about your matter and we'll walk through exactly what would and wouldn't leave your machine.
