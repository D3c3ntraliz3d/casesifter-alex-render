Built for the AI-fabrication era

Generative tools have made it fast to produce a convincing bank statement, utility bill, or affidavit from scratch — or to quietly edit a real one. A fabricated exhibit doesn't have to look sloppy anymore to be fake. CaseSifter's pipeline is built to catch the traces that kind of editing leaves behind, even when the result looks clean to the eye.

AI-edited or generated images

Error Level Analysis and copy-move detection flag localized recompression and cloning consistent with inpainting or generative edits — not just old-fashioned scissors-and-glue splicing.

Fabricated or overlaid text

OCR-vs-text-layer mismatch and invisible/micro-text checks catch AI-inserted or overlaid text that doesn't match what's actually printed on the visible page.

Fluent but invented narratives

Contextual logic cross-checks entities, dates, and amounts against the rest of the case file and public record — catching a well-written but fabricated document whose details don't hold up once compared against everything else on file.

CaseSifter flags indicators for attorney review — it does not determine whether a document was AI-generated. Decision support only.
